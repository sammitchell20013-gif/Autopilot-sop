### **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**

**Current (Organic Growth):**
- Cost: Time + $0 ads
- CAC: ~$0-10/customer

**With Paid Ads (Later):**
- Google Ads: ~$50-100/customer
- Facebook Ads: ~$30-80/customer
- Target CAC: **<$100/customer**

**Rule of Thumb**: CAC should be **< 3 months of revenue per customer**
- If CAC = $100, customer must pay at least $33/mo ✅

---

### **Lifetime Value (LTV)**

**Average Customer Lifetime**: 18-24 months (industry avg)

| Plan | Monthly Price | Lifetime (18mo) | LTV |
|------|--------------|-----------------|-----|
| Starter | $29 | 18 months | $522 |
| Pro | $79 | 24 months | $1,896 |
| Business | $199 | 36 months | $7,164 |

**LTV:CAC Ratio Target**: **3:1 or higher**
- If LTV = $522, CAC should be < $174 ✅

---

### **Churn Rate**

**Target**: <5% monthly churn (industry standard: 5-7%)

**Impact of Churn:**
- 5% churn = avg customer stays 20 months
- 3% churn = avg customer stays 33 months (better LTV)

**How to Reduce Churn:**
- Excellent onboarding
- Regular product updates
- Responsive support
- Customer success check-ins

## 🚨 Risk Factors & Mitigation

### **Risk 1: High Churn**
**Impact**: Lose customers faster than you gain them  
**Mitigation**:
- Excellent onboarding
- Regular feature updates
- Proactive support
- Customer success team

### **Risk 2: OpenAI Cost Increases**
**Impact**: Your margins shrink  
**Mitigation**:
- Pass costs to customers
- Use cheaper AI models (GPT-4 mini)
- Batch processing to reduce costs
- Consider alternatives (Anthropic, open-source)

### **Risk 3: Competition**
**Impact**: Big players enter market  
**Mitigation**:
- Niche focus (specific industries)
- Superior customer service
- Network effects (team collaboration)
- Move fast, iterate quickly

### **Risk 4: Economic Downturn**
**Impact**: Customers cut spending  
**Mitigation**:
- Focus on ROI in marketing
- "Save money" positioning (reduce training costs)
- Flexible pricing
- Annual contracts (more predictable)
